Privacy and use information

Use only non-sensitive operator-owned conversation. This is an independent faculty-led project, not an official James Madison University service, student service, research consent or IRB-approved program.

Complete text questions, successfully recognized voice transcripts and AI text answers are saved locally, together with source identities, feedback, diagnostic events and Owner review. Owner may review records for debugging, quality and suspected misuse. No fixed text-retention deadline or automatic deletion schedule has been selected. New conversation clears active model context; it does not delete saved history. Opening history does not call a model.

Microphone recordings and generated spoken audio are temporary and are not archived in history or backups. Local is the default. Explicit eligible OpenAI / Compare sends the applicable active non-sensitive conversation context to OpenAI and uses API credits. Saving or reviewing history does not send it to Cloud. Course grounding remains Local-only.

Use only an operator-controlled entry. Do not submit credentials, student or research records, or sensitive institutional data. Storage encryption and an extra dashboard password have not been established by this feature. Future participant policy requires a separate decision.
